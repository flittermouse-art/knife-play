## Page 7 of 8

  **Caption:**

  > And then Terra _glowed_, golden joy literally shining around the knife in her heart, blooming, spreading, tickling her scalp and her toes.
    She cooed and sobbed and laughed, the sweat on her face, her whole face sparkling.
    The ropes fell slack and slithered away.
    The knife shrank and slipped out and tinkled on the floor.
    The wound in her breast closed, blood slowed to a trickle.
    Terra sighed her deepest satisfaction.
    And the blindfold lifted.
    Terra lay still.
    Then opened her eyes so she could adore Luna's face.
